Cheese plate model

INNOREL CP10 · 200 × 100 × 10 mm · 6061-T6

Modeled from INNOREL's dimensioned drawing (Amazon image 71V4ad30vkL). Printed dimensions are used directly: slot width 6.4, slot counterbore 10.3, slot pitch 24.7 and 74.3, slot rows 42.3, end slots 27. Hole centers without a printed dimension were scaled from the drawing at 6.13 px/mm and made symmetric; after that the counts match the drawing exactly. Threaded holes are drawn at tap drill size.

The four highlighted 1/4-20 holes at (±49.5, ±30.85) carry the adapter. X = ±49.5 follows from printed dimensions; Y = ±30.85 is scaled from the image, so measure the 61.7 mm spacing on your plate before printing. The Ø7.0 clearance holes absorb ±0.3 mm.
