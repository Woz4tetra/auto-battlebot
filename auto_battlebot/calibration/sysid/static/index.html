    How much is characterized

    Parameter confidence ring

    One sector per plant parameter, five ticks each. A tick lights for every fifth of the way the parameter's uncertainty has come down to its target. Uncertainty is the Cramér-Rao bound from the Fisher information, widened by the fit residual when the model cannot reach the tag noise. Amber ticks mark a parameter this floor is too small to finish. ↑ ↓ pick a parameter.

    Plant Coverage Monitor

    Drive however you like. The tool reads the sticks, the ESP-NOW telemetry and the tag, labels each thing you do, drops anything that touched a wall, and refits as you go. It never sends a command and never asks you to start anything.

    Plant structure and priors follow auto_battlebot/control/plant.py; the fit input is the motor command the ESCs got, and every prediction runs the sticks through the mirror of the firmware mixer and yaw loop (auto_battlebot/control/firmware_mixer.py). This page only listens. The final calibration is the offline fit on the session's MCAP.
