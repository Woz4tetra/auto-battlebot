Measurements behind the pose bias term in `auto_battlebot/perception/sensing_model.py`.

## Sensing model bias

### `perception/sensing_model.py`

#### Along-track bias

The along-track bias differs with direction of travel. On the 2026-10-15 sysid sessions it was
+4 mm going forward and +13 mm in reverse above 1.5 m/s.
