# Mass property lumping details

## Mass property sources

- Total mass and inertia: Onshape "Mass and section properties" on the whole Mr Stabs Mk2 assembly, screenshot 2026-09-25 23:10 (`onshape_export/onshape_mass_properties_2026-09-25.png`). Assembly frame, mm and g mm^2, inertia about the COM. Unlike the URDF export, this includes the Onshape mass overrides for the motors, ESCs, flight controller, batteries and switch.
- Gear ratio: Repeat Robotics Compact 1806 gearmotor (repeat-robotics.com/products/repeat-compact-1806).
- Rotor inertia: CAD integration of the rotor: steel flux ring, 14 N52 magnets, aluminum end cap, steel shaft.
